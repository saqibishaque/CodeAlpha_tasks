Provocative Art from the Heart of Pakistan.

FISO is an independent gallery platform interrogating contemporary South Asian narratives. From radical neo-miniatures on handmade Wasli to kinetic truck art pop and Indus alluvial ceramics, we champion art that challenges conformity and sparks critical debate.

Curatorial Framework

The Four Contemporary Pillars

Explore All Categories
01

Modern Miniature

Subverting classical Mughal court aesthetics using single-hair wasli techniques to confront post-colonial realities.
02

Truck Art Evolution

Elevating highway chamakpatti and enamel craftsmanship into monumental indigenous pop art.
03

Calligraphic Abstraction

Kinetic gestural brushstrokes with genuine Afghan lapis lazuli and gold dust emancipated from classical orthodoxy.
04

Indus Heritage

5,000 years of unbroken ceramic memory using alluvial silt and wood-fired pit kilns from Harappa and Mohenjo-Daro.
